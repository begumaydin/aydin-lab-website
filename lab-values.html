Lab Values

Rigorous Science & Creativity

We ask bold, creative questions and pursue rigorous answers with curiosity, dedication, and scientific excellence.

Respect & Belonging

We cultivate a respectful environment where every lab member feels a sense of belonging and where their ideas, perspectives, and contributions are valued.

Individual & Collective Growth

We grow as scientists and as people by learning from one another, collaborating generously, supporting each other, and enjoying the process.
